      Assumptions and method

      Expected return = Σ wᵢ·μᵢ; risk = √(wᵀΣw) with Σᵢⱼ = ρᵢⱼ·σᵢ·σⱼ; Sharpe = (return − risk-free) ÷ risk. The suggestion maximises expected return subject to: weights ≥ 0 and summing to 100%; each asset-class cap; non-US share of equities within the range; risk ≤ the maximum; and, if switched on, the bad-case cone value at retirement ≥ the floor. Same objective as the Excel Solver "Target σ" run. Solved in the browser with an augmented-Lagrangian projected-gradient method. Assumptions: nine course asset classes from the professor's Efficient Frontier workbook (Bloomberg monthly data); REITs (FTSE Nareit All Equity, Nareit) and Cash (3-month T-bills, FRED TB3MS) estimated with the professor's method. Current portfolio = look-through of the client's existing target-date fund (fact sheet 30 Jun 2026; Russell growth shares from Bloomberg, 28 Oct 2026).
